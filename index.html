Screen check + three tests · about 10 minutes

Color Vision Lab

Three short tests that look at your color vision from different angles. Together they point to which cone type is affected (red, green or blue) and roughly how strongly.

Start with step 0, the screen check: screen settings and room light can change your score. Even after it, a monitor isn't a calibrated clinical instrument, so treat the result as a well-informed guess and confirm it with an optometrist (an anomaloscope test settles the exact type).

Step 0 · Screen check · about 2 minutes

Set up your screen

The tests measure very small color differences, so your screen and room matter. A web page can't read or change your display settings, so go through this list once on each device and tick each item as you go. Items that don't apply to your device can be ticked too.

4 · Check what you see

Each strip has five squares, numbered from faintest (1) to clearest (5). On a well-set screen you can see at least squares 3, 4 and 5 in both strips.

Which of these grays looks the most neutral, without any yellow, pink or blue tint?

Light or dark mode doesn't matter: while a test is open the page switches to the same mid gray in both, so your eyes adjust the same way on every run. The theme button is paused until you leave the test.

Test 1 of 3 · Cambridge-style threshold test

Find the gap in the C

A ring shaped like the letter C is hidden in the dots. It differs from the background only in color; brightness is scrambled so you can't use it as a clue. Say which way the opening faces. When you can't see a C, say so instead of guessing. The color gets fainter as you succeed and stronger when you miss, separately along the red, green and blue confusion lines.

Example · opening faces right

Keys: ← ↑ → ↓ for the opening, Space for "can't see".

About 45–65 trials.

Test 2 of 3 · Farnsworth D-15 style

Put the colors in order

Starting from the fixed reference cap on the left, pick the cap that looks most similar to the last one you placed, and keep going until the tray is empty. Click a placed cap to send it back. People with a color deficiency tend to jump across the color circle along their own confusion line, and the direction of those jumps reveals the type.

Which strip looks the same?
